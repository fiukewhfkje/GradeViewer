## Current behavior

- SwiftUI, SwiftData, Keychain, WebKit, URLSession, WidgetKit, and the canonical local GradeCore package. No analytics, paid tier, ads, or required backend.
- Foreground entry refreshes both providers concurrently. Overlapping calls coalesce; provider results publish independently; section reads use bounded concurrency. Pull-to-refresh works on Grades, course detail, and full assignments. A cancellable one-minute refresh runs only while active.
- The latest user preference covers all foreground grade views with an opaque loading gate until every connected provider delivers fresh data. A failure retains cached records internally and shows retry/reconnect. Profile and sheet dismissal remain accessible. Manual checks after a successful foreground refresh keep the current screen visible.
- Home freshness and assignment rows share the latest scored timestamp across linked provider variants. Schoology-only recent work is retained. Dates re-evaluate after refresh/foreground. Checkmarks last less than **24 hours**, per the user’s final correction. Ages over 30 days show completed 30-day months.
- Campus cookies rotate back into Keychain after successes and failures. Expired/deleted/foreign cookies are removed. A rejected session may revisit its saved same-origin Student portal once; normal reconnect restores saved Campus cookies into the nonpersistent login view. Server-rejected sessions still require normal district sign-in.
- Unique course matching uses compatible explicit metadata, matching period type/date boundaries, or matching name words plus two distinct assignment identities. Physical section pairs then align empty quarters, exams, and semester Finals. Conflicting teachers, years, kinds, or duplicate candidates remain separate. No manual matching controls.
- One current home card per physical class. Period selection appears only as compact, horizontally scrollable pills in View All Assignments: quarters, semester-wide assignments, and exams. The user rejected the separate period menu; it and the overview picker were removed. The Course Grade shows the associated semester’s reported overall. The class overview stays on the current quarter; assignment pills scope the full list and its semester summary. Semester union rows never become fabricated calculator contributions.
- Schoology wrapper and graded-node IDs are both validated on scoped detail reads. Missing titles reuse previously resolved metadata; unknown catalog kinds require explicit identity and compatible metadata. Inaccessible titles use a neutral label while preserving valid scores.
- Repeat Schoology reads use its documented combined user-grade list and previously verified enrollment aliases. New/unresolved owners use section reads. Optional period metadata is cached for one hour without extending its own expiry; new period IDs renew it. No grade scores are served from that metadata cache.
- Calculated graph prefixes are anchored to the reported baseline, avoiding a fake 0.01 endpoint transition. Observed history stays separate; tooltip shows grade/date only. Local What-If applies only a reliable delta, including semester weights only when they reproduce the reported semester total.
- Widgets read a protected App Group summary and make no provider requests. App/widget source installation is validated; physical Home Screen placement remains a user action.
